# Review summary (public, rating-free)

Internal AI-assisted review (editor, methodology, domain, devil's-advocate and perspective roles), an AI-assisted adversarial review by OpenAI gpt-5.6-sol, and a verification re-review. This is not peer review, independent reproduction or formal verification.

## Substantive objections and dispositions

1. **Scope of the conjecture claim.** Van der Kamp states his results for generic parameters; it must be precise which reading is proved. *Disposition:* Definition 1.2 distinguishes generic (G), one-sided (O) and all-parameter (A) readings; Conjecture 12 is reading (G); the paper proves (O), which implies (G). The source's definition of LV-equivalence and its procedure are quoted.
2. **Conjugacy to a special member of another family.** Early drafts covered only generic-to-generic conjugacy. *Disposition:* Lemma 3.5 and Theorem 3.6 (one-sided rigidity) added.
3. **Wording about the all-parameter extension.** The adversarial review showed that Proposition 3.8 refutes only the all-parameter extension (A), not Conjecture 12; at the displayed matrix both descriptions have the same exceptional support graph. *Disposition:* all wording now says that the extension fails and the conjecture is unaffected.
4. **Injectivity step in Theorem 3.6.** The sentence "two distinct triangles share at most one element" did not by itself give injectivity. *Disposition:* repaired with the cycle argument using independence of the basis.
5. **Proposition 2.5(i) formula.** When a directed difference vanishes, the formula for the edge polynomial degenerates to a coordinate ray. *Disposition:* statement rewritten to list the remaining edge rays explicitly.
6. **Unqualified "one polynomial per edge".** Holds only on the generic set. *Disposition:* Theorem 2.3 is an equivalence with Delta_T != 0; Remark 2.4 gives a counterexample off the generic set.
7. **Proof details and positioning.** Core-forest bijection and group quotient formalised; Whitney/Jung cited; identification with the source's counts stated as resting on its procedure (open in general); related work added (Evripidou–Kassotakis–Vanhaecke; Kahan-map tree paper; classical Darboux theory; constant-b case; Table 7 versus Figure 15 at n = 6).
8. **Constant growth rate.** The adversarial review noted that the tangent-field, recognition and Kahan statements change when b != 0. *Disposition:* Remark 2.6 states the modified forms.

No counterexample to any theorem on the generic set was found.
