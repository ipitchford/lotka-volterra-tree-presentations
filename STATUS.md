# Status

**Unrefereed AI-assisted research candidate**, version 1.0.0-candidate, 22 October 2026. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23564794](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23564794).

- Every theorem has a complete written proof in [lotka_volterra_tree_classification.pdf](lotka_volterra_tree_classification.pdf), valid for all finite trees. No proof assistant was used.
- Finite exact computations (Python, SymPy) check the statements for small trees; they confirm instances, not the general theorems.
- One example (van der Kamp's 13-parameter system) is exploratory: five sample points only.
- Open problems are listed in Section 10 of the paper and in [CLAIMS.json](CLAIMS.json).
- Van der Kamp's Conjecture 12 concerns generic parameters. It is proved here, in a stronger one-sided form. The all-parameter extension fails (Proposition 3.8); that does not affect the conjecture.

Not established: external peer review, independent reproduction, formal verification, exhaustive priority. See [ASSURANCE.md](ASSURANCE.md).
